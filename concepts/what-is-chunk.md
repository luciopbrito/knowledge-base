# what is chunk

A chunk is a piece of something bigger. In other words, imagine that you have a large amount of content from PDFs, Docs
etc. And you need to summarize the content into smaller content and saving them in a vector database. Then, the small
piece of them it will be called chunk.

## what is overlap

The overlap concept is a strategy to copy the ending content of a previous chunk into the next chunk. The goal is to
keep each chunk related another one.
