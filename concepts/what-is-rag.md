# what is RAG

RAG means Retrieval-Augmented Generation. The proposal of RAG is to extract the relevant content from data collection
(e.g. PDFs, Images, Docs). After adding the content to the context, send the context to the LLM. Nowadays, this approach
has become the biggest skill that the IT market has been looking for.

The LLM has changed the way we collect data. Worldwide enterprises have been applying these technologies to their
products.
